THE PROBLEM

The silent kind of broken.

Your client's Shopify-to-QuickBooks Zap still shows green in their Zapier dashboard. It's been "on" for three weeks straight. It also hasn't actually run once since a filter change on the 14th — and nobody finds out until the client asks where last month's invoices went. Zapier didn't error. Nothing broke loudly. It just went quiet, and quiet doesn't show up anywhere you'd think to look.
